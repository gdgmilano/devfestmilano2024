Bio

I'm Yonatan, a seasoned startup professional with a trajectory from developer to founder. At Monday.com, I led the development of the Public API and formed a pivotal mobile team, and also co-founded the innovative genetics startup KolGene. Recently, I've been focusing on Generative AI, leading its integration at Monday.com. Starting as a solo developer, I built a team that successfully launched AI solutions now enhancing our customer experience. Beyond my professional role, I'm an avid speaker, traveling globally to share insights on Generative AI, software innovation, and personal development. These engagements reflect my passion for knowledge exchange and continuous learning. Additionally, as a co-leader of Android Academy, I advocate for gender diversity and technological education, combining my professional expertise with a commitment to community enrichment and mentorship.
